# Security policy

This policy covers every repository in the ImpossibleComputing organization that does not have its own `SECURITY.md`.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Report it privately through GitHub: open the affected repository, go to **Security → Report a vulnerability**, and fill in the form. Don't open a public issue, pull request or discussion.

Include:

- the repository, and the commit or release it affects;
- what an attacker can do, and what they need first;
- steps to reproduce it, or a proof of concept;
- any logs, request IDs or run IDs (`run_…`) that show it.

## What happens next

1. The report is acknowledged.
2. The issue is reproduced and its severity assessed.
3. A fix is prepared, and an advisory is published once the fix ships. Reporters are credited unless they ask not to be.

## Scope

In scope: code in this organization's repositories.

Out of scope: findings from automated scanners with no demonstrated impact, social engineering, denial of service, and third-party services.
